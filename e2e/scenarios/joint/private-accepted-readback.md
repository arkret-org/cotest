# Private accepted readback through the real UI

The controller registers at Coauth, authorizes Inkson, creates the principal and
first Device through identity onboarding, and configures recovery in the UI.
There is no session injection, recovery override, raw protocol writer or
preinstalled plaintext. Email delivery and the deterministic model text are
controlled; Coauth, Coland, Inkson, Savfox and all cryptographic operations are real.

The controller provisions and pairs the Agent, adds it to a Realm and sends
three distinct private requests from one source card. Authentic WebSocket
handshake/control frames pass through, while all live data frames are withheld
and HTTP Account/Event/Signal subscriptions are blocked. Existing HTTP streams
must be retired before the first private Send. Independent resources remain
available. Each original must be accepted once, its authenticated response must
be visible as plaintext, and the installed product fold must bind those exact
request/response identities without another message or page refresh.

After the three replies, a real reload obtains a fresh authoritative baseline
and restores the same exchanges and MLS state without resubmitting writes.
This consumer scenario does not claim the complete cross-member privacy,
third-party execution, large-history cost or 0900 joint-full matrices.
